# voice.md — Cheikh Sambakhe

## Tone (3-5 adjectives)

1. **Calm** — no hype, no exclamation-point energy. State the thing, let it land.
2. **Authoritative** — you've done the work, so you say it once and move on. No hedging ("I think," "maybe," "kind of").
3. **Direct** — sentence one gets to the point. No warm-up, no throat-clearing.
4. **Grounded** — proof comes from one real, mundane moment (a call, a DM, a redesign), not abstract claims.
5. **Approachable** — the tone of a friend who fixes your problem, not a brand announcing itself. Confident, never corporate.

---

## Word Rules

### NEVER
- Em dashes
- "I'm thrilled to announce" / "I'm excited to share"
- Passive voice — say "built," not "was built by"
- Adjective stacks with no number behind them
- Fluff intros before the hook

### ALWAYS
- Specific numbers ("200k+," not "thousands")
- Arrow lists (→) for features or steps
- Short paragraphs, white space between them
- Active voice, present tense for results
- "Here's how it works" as the pivot from hook to proof
- A CTA with a trigger word ("Comment 'FUNNEL'")

---

## Sentence Structure

- **Hook:** one declarative sentence. Bold claim, specific detail, or contrarian take.
- **Proof:** a two-sentence story — the mundane moment + the result.
- **Lists:** → only. Never bullets, never numbered lists.
- **Paragraphs:** 1–3 lines max. A single-line paragraph is fine — often better.
- **Transitions:** keep them plain — "here's how it works:" / "here's what changed:" / "here's the thing:" / "this is why:"
- **Closing line:** broaden the implication before the CTA — zoom out from the specific story to the bigger point.
- **CTA:** one line. Action verb + trigger word. Nothing more.

---

## Platform-Specific Rules

### LinkedIn / Instagram (long-form posts)
Hook line → white space → 2-sentence proof → arrow list → credibility anchor → implication → CTA
- Numbers go in the credibility anchor, not the hook.
- The hook has to work standalone — if someone reads only that line, it should still hit.

### Short captions / comments
- One idea. Two or three sentences max.
- No lists. No CTA. Just the observation.

### DMs
- Sound like a person, not a brand.
- No formal openers ("Hope this finds you well").
- Get to the point in sentence one.

### Scripts / Video
- Same hook structure, but spoken out loud — avoid tongue twisters and abstract nouns.
- "You" replaces "I" when teaching — shift the perspective onto the viewer.
